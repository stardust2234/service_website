## 5. Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law, we need a lawful basis for processing personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on:

**Contract**: where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform a contract with you.

This may apply when you request a quotation, discuss engaging our services or become a client.

**Legitimate interests**: where processing is reasonably necessary to operate and protect our business, communicate with prospective or existing clients, maintain records or respond to enquiries, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.

**Legal obligation**: where we need to retain or process information to comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting or regulatory requirements.

**Consent**: where we specifically ask you for consent for a particular use of your information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
