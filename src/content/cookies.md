## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites can store on your device when you visit them.

Similar technologies, including local storage, may also be used to provide website functionality.

Some technologies are necessary for a website or service to work, while others may be used for purposes such as analytics, personalisation or advertising.

Where cookies or similar technologies are strictly necessary to provide functionality you have requested or to operate the website securely, they may be used without asking you to accept optional cookies.
